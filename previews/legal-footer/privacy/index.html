Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

Muraliste (“we”) helps muralists turn artwork into a paint palette and estimate. This policy explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it. Questions go to privacy@muraliste.com.

Using Muraliste without an account

As a guest, your artwork is analyzed in your browser. It is not uploaded to our servers. Settings and merge choices you save as a guest are stored in your browser’s local storage on that device only; clearing your browser data removes them.

What we store when you sign in

  • Account details from your sign-in provider (Google, and in future Apple, Facebook, or Adobe): your name, email address, and the provider’s account identifier. We never receive or store your provider password.
  • Session records that keep you signed in, which can include your IP address and browser type.
  • Projects you choose to save: the palette and estimate data, a reduced-size copy of your image, and a small thumbnail. Your browser shrinks and re-encodes the image before it leaves your device. We never store the original full-resolution file, and photo metadata (EXIF, including location) is removed first.

We use this data only to run Muraliste for you. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or use your artwork to train AI models. Muraliste has no analytics or advertising trackers.

Cookies and local storage

  • Session cookie and security (CSRF) cookie: set only when you sign in; needed to keep you signed in safely.
  • Local storage: your light/dark theme, Pro settings, and guest merge choices.

All of these are needed for the site to work, so we don’t show a consent banner.

How long we keep it

  • Saved projects are kept until you delete them or your account. We never delete projects for age or inactivity.
  • Going over the free plan’s project limit makes your projects read-only. It never deletes them.
  • A deleted project goes to Trash and can be restored for 14 days. After that it is permanently erased, including its image and thumbnail.
  • When you delete your account, there is a 30-day window in which you can cancel. After that, all your projects, images, sessions, and your account record are permanently erased.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete projects any time from Projects.
  • Export or delete everything: see Delete your data.
  • Access or correct your data, or ask any privacy question, by emailing privacy@muraliste.com. Depending on where you live (for example, under GDPR or California law) you may have additional rights. We honor these requests for every user.

Who processes data for us

  • DigitalOcean: application and database hosting.
  • Cloudflare: DNS, content delivery, security, and email forwarding for our contact addresses.
  • Your sign-in provider (Google, Apple, Facebook, or Adobe): confirms your identity under their own privacy policy.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Image files are checked on our servers without being opened by image-processing libraries. Please report security issues to security@muraliste.com.

Children

Muraliste is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. If a change materially affects how your saved data is used, we will show a notice in the app first.

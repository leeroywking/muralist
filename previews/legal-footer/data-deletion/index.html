Delete your data

Guests

If you never signed in, we have nothing stored about you. Your guest settings live only in your browser; clear this site’s data in your browser settings to remove them.

Delete individual projects

Open Projects and delete the project. It moves to Trash for 14 days, so you can restore it if that was a mistake, and is then permanently erased.

Export everything or delete your account

Email privacy@muraliste.com from the email address on your account. Say whether you want a copy of your data first. In-app buttons for this are on the way.

After an account deletion request there is a 30-day window in which you can change your mind. Then all your projects, images, sessions, and your account record are permanently erased.

Signed in with Facebook, Google, Apple, or Adobe?

Removing Muraliste from your provider’s connected-apps settings stops future sign-ins, but it does not delete what Muraliste has stored. Use the steps above for that.
